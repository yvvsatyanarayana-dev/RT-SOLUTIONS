Our enterprise division builds high-performance web, mobile, and cloud applications for businesses. Our academic division focuses on empowering Degree and B-Tech students through real-time project development and intensive technical training.

Evolution

The chronological growth of RT Solutions.

Foundation

Initial Setup & Core Division

RT Solutions was established in Kakinada with a primary focus on developing custom web solutions for local enterprises, utilizing pure HTML/CSS and early backend frameworks.

Expansion

Academic Training Division Launch

Recognizing the gap between academic curriculum and industry requirements, we launched our academic wing. Mentoring B-Tech students to write production-ready code.

Modernization

Cloud & Microservices Shift

Transitioned our enterprise offerings to fully cloud-native architectures. Adopted AWS, Docker, and React/Next.js as standard deliverables for high-tier clients.
